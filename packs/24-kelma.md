## 100

###
**Question:** How many letters are in the Arabic alphabet?
**Answer:** Twenty-eight

## 200

###
**Question:** What is a word that reads the same forwards and backwards called?
**Answer:** A palindrome

## 300

###
**Question:** What is the term for two words that sound alike but differ in meaning?
**Answer:** Homophones

## 400

###
**Question:** What do you call the three-letter core that Arabic words are built from?
**Answer:** The root

## 500

###
**Question:** What is the name for a word borrowed unchanged from another language?
**Answer:** A loanword

## 600

###
**Question:** What is the word for a saying the whole family repeats?
**Answer:** A proverb
