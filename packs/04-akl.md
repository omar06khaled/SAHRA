## 100

###
**Question:** Which green dish has basically every Egyptian household convinced that **their** version is the correct one?
**Answer:** Molokhia

## 200

###
**Lead:** Rice. Lentils. Macaroni. Chickpeas. Tomato sauce. Crispy onions. A dish looked at every carb in the kitchen and said **“aywa, kollo.”**
**Question:** What are we eating?
**Answer:** Koshari

## 300

###
**Lead:** That garlic-and-coriander mixture sizzling in ghee right before it hits the pot of green soup has a name.
**Question:** What is it?
**Answer:** Ta2leya

## 400

###
**Lead:** Wara2 3enab, kousa, kromb, eggplant — the vegetable keeps changing, but traditional Egyptian **mahshi** keeps coming back to one main filling.
**Question:** What is it?
**Answer:** Seasoned rice

## 500

###
**Lead:** Egyptian ta’ameya has a very important difference from the chickpea falafel common elsewhere in the region.
**Question:** What legume is Egyptian ta’ameya traditionally based on?
**Answer:** Fava beans (fool)

## 600

###
**Lead:** Egypt’s favourite carb-on-carb street food is thoroughly Egyptian now, bas its ancestry is commonly linked to the Indian dish **khichdi**.
**Question:** What two staple ingredients form the basic combination behind khichdi?
**Answer:** Rice and lentils
