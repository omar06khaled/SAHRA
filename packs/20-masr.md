## 100

###
**Lead:** Awel ma el sef yegy, el 3eila kollaha tet5ene2 3ala nafs el mawdoo3: **el Sa7el walla el So5na?**
**Question:** El Sa7el 3ala anhy ba7r?
**Answer:** El Ba7r El Abyad / El Motawasset
**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Coast_(Egypt)

###
**Lead:** Ma2aber el fara3na elli el kol ra7laha re7la marra, w el morshed beye2ool nafs el kalam **lel mara el meya**.
**Question:** Wadi El Molook fe anhy madina?
**Answer:** Lo2sor / Luxor
**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valley_of_the_Kings

###
**Lead:** Ay 7ad gay men barra lazem yenzel yeshtery 7agat men hena, w yefasel fel se3r, **w yeshtery bel ghaly bardo**.
**Question:** Eh esm el souk elli gamb sayedna El Hussein?
**Answer:** Khan El Khalili
**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khan_el-Khalili
